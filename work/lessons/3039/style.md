# Style profile (أسئلة الوحدة 4 درس 5 - القدرة)

- Source: ~64 essay problems (most with numeric answers in حصان / كم/س / ث.كجم) plus MCQ blocks (6, 50, 65) with 4 choices (أ، ب، ج، د).
- Forms:
  - Complete the statement: "المعدل الزمني لبذل الشغل يسمى ......"، "1 ث.كجم.م/ث يساوي ......"، "جميع الوحدات تصلح لقياس القدرة ما عدا ......".
  - Direct calculation: power = ق × ع on level road at max speed with resistance per ton; unit conversions (كم/س × 5/18، ÷ 75 للحصان، 735 وات).
  - Inverse: find max speed, resistance per ton, mass of car, from power.
  - Inclined plane: ق = م ± و جاهـ, up/down at max speed, same power; resistance constant, ∝ ع, ∝ ع².
  - Vector/calculus: القدرة = ق·ع with vector displacement functions; work from power by integration; average power over interval; finding unknown constants.
  - Energy: constant power from rest, ½ ك ع² = القدرة × ن.
  - Concept: proportionality (القدرة ∝ ن for constant force from rest; ع₁ vs ع₂ comparison).
- Stem length: 1-4 lines, full physical scenario with numbers and units.
- Distractors: numbers from common mistakes (forgetting 5/18, using 9.8 instead of 75, multiplying instead of dividing, sign of و جاهـ, using kg instead of ث.كجم), near values, swapped units (جول vs وات).
- Difficulty mix: ~25% recall/concept, ~50% one/two-step calculation, ~25% multi-step (inclines, proportional resistance, calculus).
