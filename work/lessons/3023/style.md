# Style profile (أسئلة الوحدة 1 درس 2 - تكامل الدوال المتجهة)
- Dominant form: "complete the statement" stems ending with "= ....... متر / م/ث" followed by 4 numeric choices (calculation, ~75%).
- Other forms: choose the correct function (س = ... / ع = ...) among 4 expressions differing by the constant of integration or sign; conceptual recall ("عند أقصى سرعة يكون ... = صفر" with choices ح، ع، س، ن); "which statement is false"; area-under-curve questions (figures - to be described in words).
- Stems are 1-3 lines: given ع(ن) or ح(ن) or ح(س) or ح(ع) plus initial conditions, ask for position, displacement, distance, velocity, time of direction change, max speed, average velocity.
- Distractors: displacement vs distance, forgetting the initial position س0 or initial velocity ع0, sign errors, using the derivative instead of the integral, evaluating at the wrong time, forgetting the 1/2 in ع^2 relations, values of partial intervals.
- Difficulty mix: ~30% direct/easy, ~50% medium (single integral + condition), ~20% hard (direction change, ح بدلالة س/ع, exponentials, two bodies).
- Units: م/ث، م/ث^2، سم/ث، متر، سم؛ time ن in seconds; variables Arabic س ف ع ح ن.
