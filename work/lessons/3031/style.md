# Style profile (تمارين 9: حركة جسمين يتدليان من طرفي خيط يمر على بكرة ملساء)
- Mostly calculation problems (≈80%): acceleration, tension, pressure on pulley, speed/distance after time, vertical distance between bodies, cutting the string, larger mass hitting the ground, finding an unknown mass from given motion/pressure/tension.
- MCQ part (اختر): short stems "فإن ... تساوي" with 4 numeric choices (often ascending: 1، 2، 3، 4 or fractions of ء), plus conceptual items (doubling masses, adding equal mass, pressure after cutting = صفر, motion type after equalising masses).
- Units mixed: كجم/م/نيوتن/ث.كجم and جم/سم/داين/ث.جم; g = 9.8 م/ث² = 980 سم/ث².
- Distractors: forgetting factor 2 in ض = 2ش, confusing ش with ض, unit errors (نيوتن vs ث.كجم, داين vs ث.جم, م vs سم), using difference instead of sum, f instead of 2f, sign errors after cutting the string.
- Difficulty mix: ~25% recall/concept, ~50% direct application, ~25% multi-step (مستويات عليا).
- Stems usually 2–3 lines and describe the full situation in words.
