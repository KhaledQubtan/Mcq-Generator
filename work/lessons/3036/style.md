# Style profile (تمارين 15 على مبدأ الشغل والطاقة)
- Forms: mostly calculation "complete the statement" stems ending with "= ...... جول / ث.كجم / م/ث"; a few conceptual/ratio items (الشغل اللازم لزيادة السرعة من ع إلى 2ع = 3ش، سقوط من 3ف يغوص 3س، نسبة السرعات بعد 4ف و 9ف)، and "which solution is correct" reasoning item.
- Contexts: free fall/vertical projection, horizontal force vs resistance, smooth/rough inclined plane (length, height, sin, friction coefficient), bullets penetrating/stopping in barriers, hammer and nail (momentum then work-energy), sinking into sand, variable force ق = f(س) (integral), two-layer barriers, ratio problems with same material.
- Stems: 2-4 lines, numeric data with mixed units (جم، طن، كم/س، سم، ث.كجم، ثقل جم) requiring conversion.
- Distractors: forgetting g (ث.كجم vs نيوتن), unit-conversion errors (جم/كجم, سم/م, كم/س), sign errors (negative work), forgetting a force component (weight on incline, or weight during sinking), using ع instead of ع^2, ratio squared vs not.
- Difficulty: ~30% direct/easy, ~50% medium multi-step, ~20% higher-order (ratios, two layers, variable force).
