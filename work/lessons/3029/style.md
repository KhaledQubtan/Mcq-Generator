# Style profile - تمارين 7 (حركة جسم على مستو مائل أملس)
- Mostly calculation problems (≈70%): given mass, inclination (angle, sin, tan as ratio), force (newton / ث.كجم / ث.جم), find acceleration+direction, reaction, force, mass, velocity, distance, time.
- Multi-step: compute acceleration then use equations of motion (ع = ع0 + ح ن، ف = ع0 ن + ½ ح ن²، ع² = ع0² + 2 ح ف); removing force -> deceleration د sin هـ; projectile up the plane under weight only.
- Force orientations: along line of greatest slope up, horizontal toward plane, inclined to the plane at an angle.
- MCQ part (اختر): conceptual recall (acceleration under weight only = د sin هـ; depends only on angle; independent of mass; normal acceleration = 0; contact force between two bodies = 0), short calculations with 4 numeric choices.
- Distractors: د cos هـ vs د sin هـ, forgetting g (ث.كجم vs نيوتن), wrong direction (لأعلى/لأسفل), sign errors, using cos instead of sin, near values, cm vs m, grams vs kg.
- Stem length: 1-3 lines; difficulty mix: ~25% recall/understanding, ~50% application, ~25% higher.
