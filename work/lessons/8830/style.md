# Style profile (اسئلة الوحدة 2 درس 9 ديناميكا 3 ث)
- Source: 14 pages, ~51 items in two sections: smooth inclined plane (22) and rough inclined plane (18), plus "higher-order" MCQs.
- Dominant form: multi-step calculation (acceleration, tension, pressure on pulley, reaction, distance/time/velocity after cutting the string, unknown mass or angle, coefficient of friction).
- MCQ items: "complete" style (فإن ... = ....) with 4 numeric choices, often near values (e.g. 14.7 / 19.6 / 7.35 / 9.8) or symbolic in ك د (1/4 ك د, 1/2 ك د, 3/4 ك د) and pressure forms (ش جذر2، ش جذر3، 2ش، ش جذر(2(1+جا هـ))).
- Stems are medium-long, describing the setup fully (mass on incline, angle, smooth pulley at the top, hanging mass / scale pan).
- Units mixed: كجم / م/ث2 / نيوتن / ث.كجم and جم / سم/ث2 / داين / ث.جم; distractors exploit unit confusion, sin vs cos, forgetting factor (1 + جا هـ), wrong direction of friction, dividing by one mass instead of the sum.
- Difficulty: mostly medium-to-hard application, few direct recall items. MCQ choices are listed ascending or in symbolic families.
