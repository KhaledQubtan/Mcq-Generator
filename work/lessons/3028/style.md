# Style profile (questions PDF: تمارين 6 - القانون الثالث لنيوتن / الحركة الرأسية لمصعد)
- Mostly calculation (≈75%): elevator floor pressure/reaction, spring-balance reading (apparent weight), rope tension of elevator, finding mass or acceleration from readings, two-reading systems (up with ج / down with ج or 2ج), max load given rope limit, multi-stage motion (accelerate / uniform / decelerate) combined with kinematics.
- Conceptual MCQ (≈25%): which state of motion gives reading less/greater than true weight, direction of motion & acceleration from true vs apparent weight, formula for reading, two-pan balance always gives true weight, forces acting on body vs between body and surface, comparison ر1 vs ر2.
- Stems: 1-3 lines, "complete" style with "......." or "فإن ... =".
- Units mix: كجم/جم/طن, ث.كجم/ث.جم/ث.طن, نيوتن/داين, م/ث² vs سم/ث² — conversions are a main source of distractors.
- Distractors: using (د - ج) instead of (د + ج), forgetting to divide by 9.8 (answer in نيوتن vs ث.كجم), not converting سم/ث² to م/ث², swapping up/down, ratio reversed, ignoring elevator mass, using velocity instead of acceleration.
- 4 choices, numbers often in ascending order.
