# Style profile (تمارين 16 على طاقة الوضع)
- Dominant form: "complete the statement" with blank (........) followed by unit; 4 numeric choices.
- Mostly calculation (~80%): ض = ك د ل, conservation of energy (free motion, smooth incline, pendulum), energy with resistance on rough incline, general definition with vectors.
- Some conceptual: direction of change (PE decreases/KE increases), relation أ = -ب, pendulum formula ك د ل (1 - جتا هـ), ratio questions.
- Distractors: forgetting g (13500 vs 132300), wrong unit (ث.كجم.متر vs جول, factor 9.8), sign errors (-205.8 vs 205.8), cm vs m and g vs kg (factor 100/1000), using wrong height, KE instead of PE, half/double values, sin vs cos.
- Stems short to medium (1-3 lines); units: جول، إرج، ث.كجم.متر، ث.جم.سم، م/ث، سم/ث.
- Difficulty: ~40% direct application, ~40% two-step, ~20% higher-order (ratios, rough planes, vectors).
